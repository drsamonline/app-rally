Four is a limit on the round

Not on the roster. A fifth player joins mid-round as a spectator and is promoted when the next round starts — they are never turned away.

The board checks itself

The total shown is the sum of every round's points, and the app checks it against its own history rather than assuming it.

Get it

Offline. A LAN transport does exist in this estate — `lan-kit`, zero dependencies, SSE and POST — but it is not wired into this app. What ships here is a same-device bus, so the room logic runs and can be tested. QR joining is not built either.
