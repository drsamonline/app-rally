Rally

Party games for everyone in the room — phones as controllers, one screen as the board. No accounts, no internet.

Open Rally

The LAN transport is not built, and there is no QR scanner. The demo runs the host and every player in one page through the same lobby rules a hotspot link would carry, and a lobby code is typed rather than scanned.

Status Built and working: a hosted lobby with a four-digit code; four players joining a round; three bundled games; rounds that open and close; a leaderboard whose total equals the sum of the per-round points; and a latecomer who joins as a spectator and is a player from the next round.
Release: Windows EXE, Android APK and Linux binary, built from this source in CI — app-latest.
Not built: the LAN transport, QR joining, and the games themselves beyond turn-based questions — the lobby, the rounds and the scoring are the parts that are real.

What it does

  • Hosts a lobby with a code, and takes four players into a round.
  • Runs rounds of three bundled games, and closes them to add up the points.
  • Shows a leaderboard whose total is the sum of the per-round points, and says so.
  • Puts a latecomer in as a spectator, and into the next round's players.

What it will not do

  • Require an account. A player is a name.
  • Break a round for a latecomer. They watch, then play.
  • Let a spectator score in the round they missed.
  • Reward speed too much — a fast correct answer is 13, a slow one 10, so the slow half of the room still plays.
  • Use the internet. No fetch, no endpoint; CSP connect-src 'none'.

When things go wrong

CaseWhat happens
Bad inputNo host name, no player name, a duplicate name, an answer with no round open, closing a round that is not open — each refused with the field named.
No networkNothing changes; there is no network code.
Storage full or unavailableNOT SAVED, and the lobby on screen is unchanged.
Corrupt saved stateReported and offered for export; never replaced with an empty lobby.

How to run it

  • Here: play/index.html, or install it from your browser's menu.
  • Offline file: open docs/play/index.html straight from disk.
  • Tests: node tests/self-tests.js.
